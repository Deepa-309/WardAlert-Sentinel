# WardAlert Sentinel: Clinical & Algorithmic Risk Register

| ID | Risk Description | Impact | Likelihood | Mitigation Strategy | Owner | Status |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **RSK-01** | **Missed Critical Alert (False Negative)**: Severe patient deterioration suppressed by over-aggressive grouping. | High (Critical) | Low | Architectural breakout rule: any P0 threshold breach forcibly breaks through grouping; automated unit tests enforce 100% preservation. | Clinical Safety Lead | **Active / Controlled** |
| **RSK-02** | **Over-Suppression**: Non-critical but clinically meaningful multi-system trends concealed within quiet episodes. | Moderate | Medium | Maximum episode duration cap (30m); periodic summary digest shown in Clinician Console; multi-signal cross-correlation. | Systems Architect | **Active / Controlled** |
| **RSK-03** | **False Reassurance during Data Gaps**: Lack of alarms during telemetry disconnect or battery outage interpreted as recovery. | High | Low | Telemetry heartbeat monitor: displays explicit warning *"Insufficient data — cannot safely determine trend"* whenever observations drop. | Biomedical Engineering | **Active / Controlled** |
| **RSK-04** | **Sensor Noise & Motion Artifacts**: Poor probe contact or patient motion causing recurrent false alarms and nurse distraction. | Low | High | Waveform Signal Quality Index (SQI) check; filters transient spikes (<5s) with low plethysmography perfusion index. | Algorithm Engineer | **Active / Controlled** |
| **RSK-05** | **Incorrect Grouping of Disparate Etiologies**: Independent pathologies on the same patient bundled inappropriately. | Moderate | Low | Strict grouping key: composite of `patientId` + `alertType` + physiological domain; orthogonal metrics never bundled together. | Clinical Informatician | **Active / Controlled** |
| **RSK-06** | **Threshold Misconfiguration by Operator**: Inappropriate adjustment of P0/P1 cutoffs leading to hazardous alarm delays. | High | Low | Strict RBAC constraint: only Administrators can update thresholds; audit log records all modifications; non-clinical disclaimer displayed. | Security & Governance | **Active / Controlled** |
| **RSK-07** | **Privacy Leak / PHI Exposure**: Unauthorized access to real patient medical data. | Critical | Negligible | Zero Real PHI Architecture: system strictly executes on 100% synthetic, pseudorandomly generated ward cohorts. | Compliance Officer | **Active / Controlled** |
| **RSK-08** | **Role Permission Failure**: Bedside nurse inadvertently overriding doctor prescriptions or silencing emergency protocols. | Moderate | Low | Server-side cryptographic role validation on all mutation endpoints; unauthorized requests return HTTP 403 Forbidden. | Backend Lead | **Active / Controlled** |
| **RSK-09** | **Poor Accessibility for Night-Shift Staff**: Low contrast or tiny typography causing misinterpretation during high-stress shifts. | Moderate | Medium | Dedicated "Simple View" accessibility toggle; WCAG 2.1 AA compliance; non-color-dependent status badges with icons + explicit text. | Frontend Lead | **Active / Controlled** |
| **RSK-10** | **Misunderstood AI Evidence**: Clinician confused by opaque mathematical metrics or ungrounded machine learning scores. | Moderate | Low | Transparent, rule-based clinical evidence engine; uses plain-language rationales and objective 15-minute physiological sparklines. | Clinical AI Lead | **Active / Controlled** |
